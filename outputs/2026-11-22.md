## Top 5 AI tools and news

1. **Pandex — official European law for AI agents.** A Hacker News launch from about 13 hours before this check points to a free beta for its legal-data API and MCP server; Pandex says it covers official EU and seven-country legislation and highest-court decisions. Its site offers a free API key. This is a practical way to study source-grounded retrieval. [Hacker News launch](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50593481) · [Pandex](https://www.pandex.io/) — Nov 22; launch timing shown on HN.
2. **Easy MCP — connect web AI to a local project.** Posted on Reddit today, this MIT-licensed tool links web AI clients to a chosen local folder through MCP. It can read, edit, and run commands, so a first trial should use a disposable folder or VM with shell execution disabled. [Reddit launch](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeCode/comments/1y2jmi9/easy_mcp_run_neon_hydrogen_astra_opus_on_your_pc/) · [GitHub and safety options](https://github.com/mrkaylabs/easy-mcp) — Nov 22.
3. **OpenRouter’s inference-provider incident disclosure.** OpenRouter said it removed three providers and took 125 endpoints out of Zero Data Retention eligibility after researchers reported provider data-handling issues; it said the findings did not show a breach of OpenRouter itself. A reminder to check every provider in a model-routing path. [OpenRouter announcement](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/) · [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50595554) — Nov 22.
4. **oovi — a desktop team of AI agents.** Product Hunt’s launch listing describes a planner delegating tasks to research, writing, and review agents. The app is free if you bring your own model key, but model/API usage may cost money. [Product Hunt launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/oovi) — listed as launching Nov 22.
5. **Webless Agents — site-grounded customer agents.** Its Product Hunt launch offers three months of its highest plan free. It builds agents from a company’s published site content for tasks such as answering questions and handing off customer requests; the offer is a limited promotion. [Product Hunt launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/webless) · [Launch details](https://hunted.space/dashboard/webless) — Nov 22.
