[OpenRouter announcement](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/) · [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50595554) — Nov 22.

[Product Hunt launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/webless) · [Launch details](https://hunted.space/dashboard/webless) — Nov 22.

## Recent background papers (outside the 24-hour window)

1. **RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering** — Tuna Han Salih Meral, Kaan Oktay, Alec Helbling, Atakan Kara, Duen Horng Chau, and Pinar Yanardag. **Submitted:** Nov 19, 2026, 18:59 UTC. **Status:** arXiv preprint; peer review not indicated. The authors find that small fixed steering vectors recover much of the reward gain of RL-tuned image and video diffusion models, suggesting post-training may often steer capabilities the model already has. **Why it matters:** A concrete, testable idea about cheaper diffusion-model post-training. **Caveat:** Results cover a limited set of image/video models and benchmarks; they are not proof that RL is broadly unnecessary. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627) · [Project page](https://rl-is-steering.github.io/)
2. **Pro² Agent: Scientific Problem Probing for Autonomous AI R&D** — Yixiong Chen, Liqian Peng, Tian Tan, Ziqian Lin, Blake Jianhang Chen, Chao Zhao, Yaojie Liu, Alec Go, and Alan Yuille. **Submitted:** Nov 19, 2026, 01:52 UTC. **Status:** arXiv preprint; peer review not indicated. The agent maintains an explicit “problem state,” tests hypotheses, and uses that structure to guide autonomous research instead of only editing a codebase. The authors report 70.7% on MLE-Bench under a 24-hour budget and 76.0% under 48 hours. **Why it matters:** It shows how planning and experiment design can be represented inside research agents. **Caveat:** The performance claims are author-reported benchmark results, not evidence of reliable autonomous science in general. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25357)
3. **AppSci-Bench: Can LLMs Build AI Agents and Make Them Better?** — Sanjari Srivastava, Manpreet Kaur, Cheng Chang, Sofía Ramós Anton, Oumaima Essamadi, Rishu Garg, Alejandro Lavale Jiménez, Marcos Caballero Yus, Mercedes García Martínez, and Peng Qi. **Submitted:** Nov 19, 2026, 01:58 UTC. **Status:** arXiv preprint; peer review not indicated. It benchmarks whether models can gather requirements, explore business infrastructure, build agents, and design evaluations across seven business domains. The paper reports that even its best tested model reaches a 59.1% mean-minimum score across three build attempts. **Why it matters:** It evaluates the engineering work needed to build a useful agent, not just the agent’s final answers. **Caveat:** The seven benchmark domains cannot represent every real deployment. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25360) · [Code](https://github.com/sanjari-orb/appsci-bench)
4. **One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning** — Yuxin Jiang, Wenqiang Sun, Jiaqi Li, Yuchao Gu, Lvmin Zhang, Ivor Tsang, and Mike Zheng Shou. **Submitted:** Nov 19, 2026, 18:58 UTC. **Status:** arXiv preprint; peer review not indicated. The method reuses a pretrained video generator to learn hidden actions and predict their effects from unlabeled video, with reported results on navigation and robot manipulation. **Why it matters:** It connects generative video models with world models and robotics. **Caveat:** Results are limited to the tasks and model settings evaluated; the paper does not establish broad real-world robot reliability. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617) · [Project page](https://showlab.github.io/o2laf/) · [Code](https://github.com/showlab/O2LaF)
5. **Online Temporal Rotations for 2 Bit KV-Cache Quantization in Video Diffusion Transformers** — Ryan Lucas, Petros Prastakos, and Rahul Mazumder. **Submitted:** Nov 19, 2026, 16:14 UTC. **Status:** arXiv preprint; peer review not indicated. It rotates adjacent video-cache vectors before 2-bit quantization and reports improved VBench subject-consistency scores over a comparison method on two video-generation models. **Why it matters:** It targets inference memory and speed constraints that limit longer video generation. **Caveat:** The quality gains are reported on particular models and benchmarks, so hardware and model transfer still need checking. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26334)
